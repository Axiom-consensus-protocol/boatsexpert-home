BayStar Hydraulic Steering Kit HK4200A-3.

BayStar Hydraulic Steering Kit HK4200A-3. Suitable for most single-outboard powered boats rated up to 150 hp including runabouts and inflatables.

Kit includes Dometic's BayStar Helm, BayStar Outboard Cylinder, 1 l of SeaStar Hydraulic Oil and cut-to-fit hydraulic tubing.

Crafted in accordance with the SeaStar Inboard Steering legacy, these BayStar Systems are specifically engineered for inboard applications with relatively low torque requirements, such as inland waterway boats and other slow-speed displacement vessels.

Installing these systems is a breeze thanks to the "cut-to-length" tubing. You can opt for complete kits or select individual components based on your needs.
