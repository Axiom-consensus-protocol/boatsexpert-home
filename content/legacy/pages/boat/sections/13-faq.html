01 Is the 19 cm draft real, or is that a marketing number?

Measured by us in demo, on a level concrete slip, with two adults aboard, the 55-L tank half-full, and a 75 Ah battery in the locker. We measured at the rear quarter — the deepest point of the hull with the engine trimmed up. The number was 19 cm. We did it three times because we couldn't believe it the first time.

The number drops to roughly 14 cm with the engine fully tilted up and one person aboard. You can wade off it.

02 Can I register and trailer this boat for SMB?

Yes. The 470 EVO DC is CE-marked, category C (coastal). It registers under registration with the standard pleasure-craft category and the registration costs about €120 in fees plus three working days. Trailer is included in the Angler and Tournament packs; on Standard it's an option (+€2,400, CE-marked, braked).

We do the paperwork for you if you want — included in the delivery service. Bring your ID and the bill of sale; leave with stickers.

03 Can I lease it through my company (PJ / SRL)?

Yes, through Allianz Țiriac Leasing or Raiffeisen Leasing as a business asset. The VAT (19%) becomes deductible if the boat is used commercially — for example, charter, sea trials, training. We can introduce you to an accountant familiar with the boat-leasing tax structure. Approval for PJ takes 5–7 working days.

04 What's actually included in the 10-year warranty?

The hull. All welds, all aluminium plates, the RAPTOR coating against delamination. Not covered: engines (Outboard Y's own 5-year warranty applies separately), batteries (3-year), electronics (manufacturer terms), and consumables (anodes, paint touch-ups, EVA flooring, life vests).

Claim process: one phone call. We send a courier to pick up the boat, repair it in Estonia or demo (whichever is faster), and return it on a trailer. No dealer signature required.

05 How fast can I take delivery if I sign today?

The stocked Standard pack — 5 working days. We pre-rig the engine, fit the gauges, mount the trailer, and run the sea trial. Five days, no surprises.

Angler and Tournament packs — 14–21 days, depending on whether Marine Nav and E-Motor stock is at our warehouse or ordered from Hamburg.

06 Do you deliver outside Demo region?

Yes — as standard. Delivery is by trailer with our driver, the price is included for the Angler and Tournament packs, and a flat €450 for Standard. Customs paperwork handled at the border.

For Hungary, Austria, Italy and Germany — we can arrange but it's not included; usually €900 – €1,800 by trailer, or you fly in and tow the boat home yourself.

07 Can I customise the configuration?

Yes. The three packs above are the most-requested combinations, but every option can be swapped. Want the F60 with a 12-inch chartplotter instead of a 9? Add €1,200. Want a Lowrance instead of Marine Nav? We have both — pricing in the quote. Want no trailer because you have one? Subtract €2,400.

Request a written quote — Alex builds the spec in our system and you'll have a PDF within 24 hours.

08 What happens if I reserve and then change my mind?

You get your €1,000 back. No paperwork, no questions. The reservation holds the specific hull for 14 days; if you walk away within that window we refund the deposit within 3 business days. We do this often — buying a boat is a serious commitment, and we'd rather have you walk away than buy something that doesn't fit.
