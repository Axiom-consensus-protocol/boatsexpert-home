Description · what's in the box

Steering you can feel.

The BayStar HK4200 is SeaStar's most-installed hydraulic steering kit for single-engine outboards up to 150 horsepower. One cylinder, one helm, two hoses, the fittings — and you're done with cable steering forever.

Mechanical cables stretch, bind, and corrode. They make the boat feel mushy at speed and exhausting on long trips. Hydraulic steering solves all of that. The HK4200 cylinder mounts on the transom in fifteen minutes; the helm replaces the old one without touching the dashboard. The fluid lines are pre-flared and fitted with O-rings — no flaring tool, no plumber's tape, no leaks.

The kit is rated for outboards up to 150 hp single, or up to 200 hp with a tie-bar between two engines. SeaStar's hydraulics use their own ATF-based fluid (HA5430 — sold separately, two litres needed for this kit). Steering effort drops to roughly 1.4 lbs at the helm, which after a season of fighting a cable, feels like a different boat.

BayStar is the budget-tier line — meaning the cylinder body is aluminium rather than the Pro line's stainless, and the hoses are nylon rather than braided steel. For freshwater and lakes, that's the right trade-off. If you boat the Black Sea, look at the SeaStar Pro kit instead.

Installation is something we do here every week. Bring the boat to our Otopeni workshop and the fit-out is free on orders over €1,500. We'll calibrate the trim, sea-trial it on the Danube, and write the maintenance schedule on a sticker inside the helm.
